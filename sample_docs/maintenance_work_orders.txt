Maintenance Work Order and Spare Parts Process (SAMPLE DOCUMENT - fictional data for demonstration only)

Work order priorities

Every work order is given a priority. P1 work orders are emergencies that threaten safety or production, and they must be responded to within 1 hour. P2 work orders must be responded to within 24 hours. P3 work orders are scheduled within 7 days and P4 work orders within 30 days.

Approvals

Work orders with an estimated cost above 5,000 USD require manager approval before work starts. Emergency P1 work may start immediately, and the approval is recorded afterwards.

Closing a work order

A work order can only be closed when the failure code, the labour hours and the parts used have been entered. The planner reviews every closed work order within 3 working days.

Root cause analysis

A root cause analysis is mandatory after every P1 failure and must be completed within 10 working days. The findings are shared with the reliability team.

Preventive maintenance

The preventive maintenance compliance target is 90 percent, measured as the share of scheduled tasks completed on time. The backlog is reviewed every week.

Spare parts

Critical spares are kept at a minimum stock level of 2 units. When the stock falls below the reorder point, the storekeeper raises a purchase request automatically.
